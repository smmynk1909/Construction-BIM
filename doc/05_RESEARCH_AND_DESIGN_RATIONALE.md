# Research and design rationale
Construction lifecycle platform | Reviewed 26 October 2026

## Evidence and interpretation
The sources below are public consultancy publications and official BIM guidance. They inform this proposal; they are not a consultancy endorsement, a complete literature review or proof that a particular software architecture will deliver a stated return. Publication dates and sector context matter. This blueprint's requirements, workflow IDs, roles, SLAs and release choices are our proposed design, not quotations from the sources.

## R01 BCG capital project digitisation
**Source:** [Unleashing the potential of Digital and AI technologies in energy large capital projects](https://media-publications.bcg.com/unleashing-potential-digital-ai-technologies-energy-large-capital.pdf), October 2023, especially printed pages 5–6 and 11–13.

**Research approach:** survey and strategic assessment of more than 50 companies spanning owners, contractors and technology vendors. The paper maps 45 use cases into six lifecycle support areas. Its energy-sector scope limits direct transfer to every construction business.

**Finding used:** early scope decisions, integrated project controls, coordinated engineering/supply, commissioning and data governance deserve attention alongside construction execution.

**Our application:** validate concept requirements and package handoffs early; make Operations and Maintenance reviewers during design; deliver workflow and data foundations before advanced AI. BR04/09; PR04/05/09; WF03/04/07. We do not adopt the paper's benefit estimates as this project's forecast.

## R02 Accenture data driven capital projects
**Source:** [Building More Value With Capital Projects](https://www.accenture.com/content/dam/accenture/final/a-com-migration/r3-3/pdf/pdf-143/accenture-industryx-building-value-capital-projects-highres.pdf), Capital Projects 2020 research, printed pages 2–5 and 22.

**Research approach:** survey of 570 owner-operator executives across 20 countries plus 140 EPC responses; expert discussions, financial-data analysis and regression analysis. The period studied and respondent industries limit generalisation; associations and modelled benefits are not guaranteed causal results.

**Finding used:** shared data alone needs executive ownership, workforce capability and incentives for collaborative use. Its CAPSTONE framework combines leadership commitment, sharing infrastructure, talent and contract incentives.

**Our application:** create domain data stewards, contractually defined submissions, field-user training and acceptance tests that cross company boundaries. BR01/06/12; PR01/02/08; FR07 and integration ownership. No published margin/return uplift is used in our business case.

## R03 Gartner operational digital twin value
**Source:** [2024 Power and Utilities Trend Improvements Needed to Deliver Value With Digital Twins](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/5147631), 31 January 2024.

**Access and method limit:** reviewed the public summary and partial overview only. Full research requires client access. The public page does not provide sufficient methodology to assess its evidence base; this is analyst guidance, not a study we independently reproduced.

**Finding used:** operational business value matters beyond visual situational awareness.

**Our application:** require defined operational decisions, service measures, freshness indicators and work-order actions before labelling a model/dashboard a useful operational twin. PR10/14; WF08/09. Simulation, prediction and autonomous action are distinct maturity steps; none is implied by a 3D viewer.

## R04 McKinsey transformation practice
**Source:** [Decoding digital transformation in construction](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/decoding-digital-transformation-in-construction), 20 August 2019.

**Research approach and limit:** practice-based assessment of construction transformations with illustrative cases; the public article is not a reproducible controlled trial or a procurement specification.

**Finding used:** focus on operational problems, collaborative use cases, changed working practices and repeatable scaling across projects.

**Our application:** select an end-to-end pilot, measure adoption and outcomes, then reuse controlled templates with project-specific variations. BR01/05/06; PR02/06/07 and the release acceptance gates. Do not buy disconnected pilots without an owner for the cross-functional outcome.

## R05 Information management and interoperability
**Sources:** [UK BIM Framework guidance on the common data environment](https://ukbimframework.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Guidance-Part-C_Facilitating-the-common-data-environment-workflow-and-technical-solutions_Edition-1.pdf); [buildingSMART standards library](https://www.buildingsmart.org/standards/bsi-standards/standards-library/); [buildingSMART Information Delivery Specification](https://www.buildingsmart.org/standards/bsi-standards/information-delivery-specification-ids/).

These are implementation guidance and standards references, not consultancy performance research. Use controlled information states and revision history, and evaluate IFC model exchange, BCF issues and IDS checks against actual authoring tools. Compliance with a named standard requires detailed review; this proposal does not claim certification. PR04/09; FR03 and WF03/07.

## How to apply a consulting approach to this company
The following is our synthesis for the next phase, rather than a claim that every firm uses the same method.
1. Diagnose actual handoff failures using project records, interviews and transaction walkthroughs across Sales, Planning, Finance, Accounting, Operations and Maintenance.
2. Baseline cycle times, reconciliations, forecast errors and service failures. Document sample sizes, missing records and project differences.
3. Define the target operating model: owners, decision rights, authority, RACI, SLAs and package-level gates.
4. Build a value/use-case backlog scored by impact, feasibility, data readiness, adoption and control risk. Explicitly include service reliability and commercial collection outcomes.
5. Prove the critical data links and integrations using a thin end-to-end pilot, including one maintained asset.
6. Compare outcomes to the baseline and to comparable work where feasible. Separate adoption effects, scope changes and technology effects.
7. Scale templates, training and support only after the process works. Validate predictive AI independently before adding decision authority.
