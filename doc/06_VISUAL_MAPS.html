Terms for application builders

BIM and CDE
BIM is structured asset information linked to models; a common data environment controls how project information is shared, reviewed and released.
BOQ and WBS
A bill of quantities describes measured scope and pricing items. A work breakdown structure divides the delivery scope into manageable packages. Their mapping is many-to-many.
PR and PO
A purchase requisition requests approval to buy. A purchase order creates the approved supplier commitment. Neither proves receipt or payment.
RACI and SLA
R performs the work, A owns its outcome, C provides input, I receives information. A service-level agreement defines expected response or decision timing and escalation.
Operations and Maintenance
Operations uses the asset to deliver its service. Maintenance preserves or restores its function. Safe return to service requires operating authority.
